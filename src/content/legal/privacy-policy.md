## 3. How we use your data

We use your data to:

- Create and operate your account, and authenticate you.
- Display your profile and content to the audience you choose.
- Deliver messages, notifications and emails.
- Show you relevant recruitments and let you apply to them.
- Pass your applications to the Organizations you apply to.
- Detect, investigate and prevent abuse, fraud, impersonation and harm to minors.
- Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
- Fix problems, monitor performance and improve the Platform.

Our lawful basis is your **consent**, given when you create an account and when you take specific actions such as submitting an application, and, where applicable, **legitimate uses** permitted under the DPDP Act, such as complying with law and preventing fraud.

You may withdraw consent at any time by closing your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

## 4. If you are under 18

Under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a **child**. Different rules apply.

**What we require**

- The minimum age to hold a Goatza account is 13.
- If you are under 18, you must have your parent or legal guardian's permission to use Goatza.
- Before you apply to any recruitment, you must give us your parent or guardian's name and contact number, and confirm they consent.
- We may contact your guardian to confirm.

**What we do not do**

- We do not track, monitor or profile children.
- We do not use behavioural targeting on children.
- We do not serve advertising to children.

**Additional protections**

- New posts by users under 18 default to a followers-only audience.
- We apply extra scrutiny to reports involving accounts belonging to minors.

Full details are in our [Youth Safety Policy](https://goatza.com/safety).

**Parents and guardians:** you may ask us to access, correct or delete your child's data, or to close their account, by writing to goatza.official@gmail.com. We will ask you to verify your identity and your relationship to the child before we act.

We are working towards the verifiable parental consent mechanism required by the DPDP Rules, 2025, ahead of the compliance date of 13 May 2027.

## 6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by our providers, which may be located outside India. Media files are distributed via a global content delivery network so they load quickly.

Cross-border transfer is permitted under the DPDP Act except to countries the Government of India specifically restricts. We monitor for any such restriction.

## 7. How long we keep it, and what deletion means

We want to be precise here, because "delete" means different things in different systems.

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| You delete a post, comment or message | It is immediately hidden from all users and removed from feeds. The record is retained internally in a deleted state and is permanently erased during periodic cleanup. |
| You close your account | Your profile stops being visible immediately. Your personal data is permanently erased within **30 days**. |
| Backups | Encrypted database backups are retained for a limited period and then overwritten. Data you have deleted may persist in a backup until it expires. |
| Content others hold | Messages you sent remain visible to the recipient. Applications already delivered remain with the Organization. |
| Legal holds | Where we are required to retain data for a legal claim, investigation or statutory obligation, we retain only what is necessary, for as long as necessary. |

Reports, blocks and enforcement records are retained after account closure in a minimised form, so that a banned user cannot simply re-register.

## 8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

- **Access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it.
- **Correct** data that is inaccurate, and complete data that is incomplete.
- **Erase** your data where it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
- **Grieve** — to raise a complaint with us, and to escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unsatisfied.

To exercise any of these, write to **goatza.official@gmail.com**. We will respond within **90 days**, and usually much sooner. We may need to verify your identity first.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

## 10. Security

We protect your data using access controls, encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, short-lived access tokens, rate limiting and restricted administrative access.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact goatza.official@gmail.com immediately.

**Data breaches.** If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected users and report it to the Data Protection Board of India within the timelines required by the DPDP Rules.
